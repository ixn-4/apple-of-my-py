What is MLBB?

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is commonly known as ML or MLBB. It is a mobile multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed and published by Moonton, a subsidiary of ByteDance. Mobile Legends was initially released in Brazil on 22 July 2016, marking the beginning of its rise in the world of mobile games.


Since its release in 2016, the game has grown significantly in popularity, particularly in Southeast Asia, where a large portion of its player base comes from. Mobile Legends surpassed 1 billion downloads, with a peak of 100 million monthly players. In 2021, the game achieved an all-time gross revenue of US$1 billion, making it one of the leading mobile games in its genre with global appeal.


The game sets two teams of five players against one another in real-time, with matchmaking taking as little as 10 seconds and matches lasting around 10 minutes. Players must defend and fight across three lanes—the top, middle, and bottom—to destroy the enemy's towers while protecting their own in a traditional battle arena setting. Players are also encouraged to strategize throughout the match, allowing them to utilize their individual skills and apply different strategies to achieve victory.


Since its release, Mobile Legends has continued to maintain its presence in the MOBA genre by continuously changing its game environment and dynamics. These adjustments allow the game to adapt to different player preferences and game styles, helping it remain relevant within the constantly changing mobile gaming industry.
